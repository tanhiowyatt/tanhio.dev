Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains what personal data we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your information under applicable laws, including the European Union's General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA).

1. Data We Collect and Host

Hosting and Server Logs: This website is hosted on Vercel. When you access the site, Vercel automatically collects certain technical infrastructure data (such as IP address, browser type, operating system, and request timestamps) to securely serve the website and prevent malicious activity. This processing is based on our legitimate interest in maintaining a secure and functional website.

Contact Information: If you choose to contact us by email (e.g., at contact@tanhio.dev), we process your email address and any information you provide solely to respond to your inquiry.

2. Cookies and Analytics

We use essential cookies strictly for core site functionality. Optional performance tracking (Vercel Analytics and Speed Insights) is disabled by default. It is loaded only if you provide your explicit consent through our Cookie Consent banner. You have the right to accept or decline tracking, and you can withdraw your consent at any time by clearing your browser cookies.

3. Your GDPR Rights (European Union)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), you have the following statutory rights:

  • Right to Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to Rectification: Request correction of any inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): Request deletion of your data when it is no longer needed.
  • Right to Restriction: Request that we temporarily limit the processing of your data.
  • Right to Data Portability: Obtain your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Right to Object: Object to the processing of your data based on legitimate interests.
  • Right to Lodge a Complaint: Submit a complaint to your local Data Protection Authority (DPA).

4. US Privacy Rights (CCPA / CPRA)

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for commercial or cross-context behavioral advertising. We only disclose technical data to our hosting provider (Vercel) as necessary to deliver the website services.

5. COPPA & Children's Privacy

This website is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in certain jurisdictions), and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If we become aware that we have collected information from a child without appropriate parental consent, we will delete it immediately.

6. Data Retention

We retain your personal data (such as email communications) only as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected or to comply with legal obligations, after which it is deleted or anonymized.

7. Contact Information

For any questions regarding this Privacy Policy or to exercise your rights, please email us at contact@tanhio.dev.
